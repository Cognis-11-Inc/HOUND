# HOUND doctrine

**Cognis 11 Inc. — specialist synthetic intelligence research**

> Give me a trail, give me a terrain, and let me hunt.

1. **HOUND is a specialist intelligence, not an AGI.** Its scope is tracking
   intrusion trails in explicitly authorized simulated environments. Canine
   tracking and human/K-9 partnerships inspire the research, not a claim of
   animal cognition or general intelligence.
2. **Rust governs the investigator's body, evidence, and operational boundaries.**
   In this slice Rust validates sensor records, restricts the collection interface,
   and appends evidence. A future learner may propose actions; authority over
   admitted actions and recorded evidence remains outside learned weights.
3. **A future custom PyTorch model learns hunting strategies.** The initial
   research target is approximately five million parameters. There is no model,
   training loop, inference integration, or LLM dependency in HOUND-001A.
4. **Observations, inferences, and hypotheses remain distinct.** A sensor report
   records a measurement claim with provenance; an inference relates evidence;
   a hypothesis proposes an origin with explicit support, contradictions, and
   status. Confidence and supported status do not create an observed fact.
   HOUND must eventually be able to conclude unresolved.
5. **Reward cannot determine truth.** Future intrinsic hunting motivation and
   handler reward may influence action selection, never manufacture evidence,
   erase contradictions, or make an attribution correct by reward alone. These
   mechanisms are research plans and are not implemented.
6. **Human Operator supervision must not corrupt evidence.** Operators choose
   authorized scope and can inspect or stop runs. Corrections should be separate,
   traceable annotations, never silent edits to recorded observations. Such an
   annotation mechanism is future work. The current journal rejects conflicting
   identities through its API; it cannot stop a host administrator editing files.
7. **All investigation stays inside explicitly authorized environments.** The
   current terrain contains inert synthetic traffic, isolated containers, and
   no external targets, exploits, malware, or credential theft. Operator-selected
   sensor endpoints must also belong to authorized environments.

The evaluator's answers are not evidence available to the investigator. Evaluation
results measure an experiment; they must not leak into a hunt as hidden labels.
Operational isolation is documented and tested separately from these design laws.
